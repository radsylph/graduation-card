# Judith Invitation

A digital invitation. Guests arrive at a wax-sealed envelope (the welcome screen) and open it to reveal the invitation.

## Language

**Welcome screen**:
The landing page (served at `/`) presenting the closed, wax-sealed envelope that guests see first.
_Avoid_: Landing page, splash screen, intro

**Invitation**:
The page (served at `/invitation`) containing the event details guests are invited to.
_Avoid_: Home, index page

**Wax seal**:
The interactive seal on the welcome screen; activating it opens the invitation.
_Avoid_: Stamp, wax stamp

**Opening**:
The act of activating the wax seal to leave the welcome screen and view the invitation.
_Avoid_: Unsealing, redirecting

**Invitation card**:
The cream parchment certificate that is the centerpiece of the invitation page.
_Avoid_: Hero card, certificate

**Guest name**:
The name of the individual guest the invitation is addressed to.
_Avoid_: Recipient, attendee

**RSVP**:
The guest's confirmation of whether they will attend.
_Avoid_: Signup, response form
